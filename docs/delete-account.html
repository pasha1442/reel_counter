Delete your Reel Counter account

Reel Counter by

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Reel Counter and tap your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Tap your name to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. For your safety you'll be asked to confirm with your password or Google first.

Without the app

Email from the address your account uses, with the subject “Delete my Reel Counter account”. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account: its email address, chosen name and account ID.
  • Your link to any Pro purchase in our systems.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records of Pro purchases, which Google Play and our billing provider keep for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Copies in our providers' backups, removed within about 30 days.

Your reel history

Your reel counts were never stored in your account; they live only on your phone. To erase them, open Profile → Delete all reel history, or uninstall the app.

Pro subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so you aren't charged again.
