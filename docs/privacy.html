1. What stays on your phone

To count reels and keep your history, the app stores on your phone:

  • One entry per counted reel: which app (Instagram or YouTube), the time, and which sitting it belonged to. These entries are deleted automatically after 90 days; after that only daily totals remain.
  • Daily totals, your streaks, your limits and your settings.

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. It is not included in your phone's cloud backup. You can erase it any time in Profile → Delete all reel history, or by uninstalling the app.

2. How counting works (Accessibility permission)

Reel Counter uses Android's Accessibility permission, only after you agree to it in the app. With it, the app can see the layout of Instagram and YouTube screens, and of your home screen only to notice when you leave. It uses this to notice when a new reel or short appears, and to show the block screen when you reach your limit.

  • Captions and account names on a reel are read for a moment, on your phone, to tell one reel from the next. They are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • The app never reads your messages, passwords or anything you type, and it ignores every other app.
  • The app doesn't use this permission to change your settings or act for you, except to cover the reels screen with the block screen you asked for.

You can switch the permission off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings; counting then stops.

3. Information that leaves your phone, and why

Only in the situations below, and only the information listed.

If you create an account (optional)

Accounts are for keeping Pro on a new phone and, when they arrive, friend battles. We use Google Firebase Authentication to run them.

InformationWhy
Email address, the name you choose, an account ID, how you signed inTo create your account and sign you in.
If you use Google sign-in: the basic profile Google shares (name, email, profile photo link)To sign you in. We don't show your photo.

When you create an account we ask your date of birth, only to check that you are 18 or older. The check happens on your phone; your date of birth isn't stored or sent anywhere. Your reel counts are not stored in your account.

If you buy Pro

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives details of your purchase, such as the plan, dates, price, currency and country. It also receives a random ID for your phone, or your account ID if you are signed in.

If you turn on crash reports (off by default)

Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes. A report contains technical details of the crash, your phone model, Android version, app version and an installation ID. It doesn't include your reel counts.

If you turn on usage stats (off by default)

Google Analytics for Firebase receives a few app events, such as “finished setup”, “opened the Pro screen” or “signed in”. With them come your phone model, Android version, language, country, approximate region and an app-instance ID. We switched off the advertising ID and ad personalisation. Usage stats never include your reel counts or anything read from the screen.

Notifications and the widget

Nudges, alerts and the widget are created on your phone. Nothing is sent to show them.

4. Who we share information with

Only the service providers named above: Google (Firebase and Google Play) and RevenueCat. They process data for us under their own privacy terms. We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

These providers may process data on servers outside India, including in the United States.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: individual reels for 90 days, daily totals and settings until you delete them or uninstall.
  • Account: until you delete it. Deletion is immediate; copies in provider backups are removed within about 30 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Usage stats: up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • see the personal information we hold about you, and correct it;
  • delete your account and its information, in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account) or as described on our account deletion page;
  • withdraw consent at any time. For example, turn off crash reports or usage stats in Profile, or switch off the Accessibility permission. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • complain to us, and if we don't resolve it, to the Data Protection Board of India or your local authority.

To do any of these, write to . Our contact for privacy questions and complaints is . We reply within 30 days.

7. Children

Reel Counter isn't meant for children under 13. Accounts are only for people aged 18 or over. Younger people can use the counting and limit features without an account, because that information never leaves the phone. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information sent to our providers is encrypted in transit. Account access is protected by Google's security systems. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small so there is less to protect. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app before they apply to you.
